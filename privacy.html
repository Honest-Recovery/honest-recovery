Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 13, 2026

At ReviveMedia & Recovery, privacy is not just a policy; it is the foundational architectural principle of HonestRecovery. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information.

1. The HonestRecovery Software

We designed HonestRecovery specifically to ensure absolute data privacy for our users and their clients. Therefore:

  • No Cloud Infrastructure: HonestRecovery operates 100% locally on your macOS machine. It does not connect to the internet to perform scans, recoveries, or extractions.
  • No Telemetry or Analytics: The software does not track your usage, record your clicks, or send crash reports to our servers.
  • No Data Exfiltration: At no point does your recovered data, parsed iOS databases, or file metadata leave your computer through our software.
  • Offline Licensing: License validation occurs entirely on-device using cryptographic key verification. The software does not "phone home" to verify your subscription.

2. Website and Purchasing Data

While the software is completely offline, our website (this site) and payment processor do collect some information necessary to facilitate sales and support:

  • Payment Information: We use third-party payment processors (such as Gumroad) to handle transactions. We do not store or process your credit card information directly. Please refer to their privacy policies for details on how they handle your payment data.
  • Email Addresses: When you purchase a license or contact support, we collect your email address. We use this strictly to deliver your license key, provide customer support, and send critical security updates. We do not sell your email address to third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

Our website may contain links to third-party sites or utilize external payment processors. This Privacy Policy does not apply to those third parties. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services you interact with.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our support team through the channels provided on your purchase receipt.
